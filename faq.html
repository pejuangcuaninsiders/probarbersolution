COMMON QUESTIONS

What areas do you serve in Bali?

We provide home service across Bali including Ubud, Canggu, Seminyak, Uluwatu, Sanur and surrounding villa areas. Travel fee may apply depending on location.

How do I make a booking?

You can book directly via WhatsApp. Simply send your location, preferred service and desired appointment time.

How early should I book?

We recommend booking at least a few hours in advance. Sunset hours and weekends are usually fully booked faster.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, bank transfer and selected digital payments depending on your location and booking type.

Are your tools hygienic?

Yes. All barber and beauty tools are cleaned and sanitized carefully before every appointment to ensure premium hygiene standards.

Do you accept group bookings?

Absolutely. We provide group grooming services for villa guests, families, weddings, events and private parties.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No special preparation needed. We bring professional equipment directly to your villa or hotel room for a complete luxury setup.

How fast is your WhatsApp response?

Our admin usually responds quickly during operational hours to help secure your appointment as fast as possible.
